Castmote is a lightweight remote for Chromecast. Paste a link — or send one to
Castmote from another app's share sheet or a castmote:// deep link — and cast it
to your TV.

Features:

* Cast arbitrary web videos (HLS/DASH), YouTube and SVT Play.
* YouTube and SVT are handed to their native TV receivers (real UI, captions,
  next-episode); other links play the raw stream on the default receiver.
* Built-in browser that sniffs playable streams on the page.
* Transport controls: play/pause, seek, skip steps, volume.
* Lock-screen / notification media control.
* Bookmark and resume casts from where you left off.
* Sign in to YouTube to cast ad-free.

Stream resolution uses an embedded yt-dlp.
